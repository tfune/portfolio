About Me

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at National University with a strong focus on embedded systems and low-level firmware development. I enjoy designing microcontroller-based systems that combine real-time software with physical hardware to solve practical engineering problems.

My projects involve developing embedded firmware in C/C++, implementing finite state machines, working with interrupts and GPIO peripherals, and interfacing with sensors, displays, and actuator-driven hardware. I am particularly interested in real-time embedded control, modular firmware architecture, and reliable hardware/software integration.

I enjoy building hands-on systems that strengthen both software and hardware engineering skills, from debugging embedded behavior and validating signals with lab equipment to designing responsive firmware for real-world devices. My goal is to continue developing practical embedded systems while expanding my experience with more advanced microcontrollers and hardware design.
