About Me

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at National University with a strong focus on embedded systems. I enjoy working close to the hardware, designing and programming microcontrollers, and integrating low-level software with sensors and real-world devices.

My projects often involve writing efficient firmware in C/C++ and interfacing with various hardware components, including sensors, actuators, and communication modules. I am particularly interested in real-time systems and optimizing resource usage to build reliable, responsive embedded solutions.

Beyond coding and hardware, I enjoy learning about power management in embedded devices, debugging complex system behavior, and exploring ways to make embedded systems more energy-efficient and robust. I strive to build practical, hands-on solutions that solve real-world problems whild deepening my understanding of hardware-software integration.
